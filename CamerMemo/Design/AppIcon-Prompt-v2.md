## 最终提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied CamerMemo app icon. Make ONLY this small compositional adjustment: translate the entire foreground camera, including lens, buttons, and its contact shadow, slightly to the RIGHT by approximately 6% of the square canvas width. Keep the camera's size, height, angle, material, colors and design unchanged. Keep the notebook exactly in its original position and size, preserve its binding, paper lines, texture, and lighting. Naturally reveal the portion of notebook/background previously covered at the camera's left edge. The camera must still overlap the lower notebook and remain comfortably inside the canvas without clipping. Preserve the blue background, square opaque canvas, and overall original appearance. No text, no additional elements, no outer rounded corner mask. Output one final square icon.
