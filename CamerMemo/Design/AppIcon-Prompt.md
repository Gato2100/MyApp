# CamerMemo 图标设计

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。原图为 1254 × 1254 不透明 PNG；AppIcon-1024.png 为 Xcode 所需的 1024 × 1024 尺寸版本。没有预先裁切圆角，系统会施加图标形状。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final iOS app icon for a private photo-to-text memo app named CamerMemo. Create one polished square 1024x1024 icon asset, not a mockup. Main composition explicitly required: a recognizable upright notebook behind, with a recognizable camera in the foreground at the lower center, partially overlapping and obscuring the lower portion of the notebook. Notebook has a few simple horizontal note lines and a subtle binding edge; camera has a bold circular lens and simple silhouette. Keep both shapes immediately legible at small home-screen sizes. Style: refined minimal dimensional illustration, crisp silhouettes, gentle depth and restrained soft shadows, sophisticated uncluttered visual. Background must be fully opaque and extend all the way to the square canvas edges; do not draw an outer rounded square or corner mask, because iOS masks the icon itself. Balanced generous inner margins, centered large emblem. No letters, words, numbers, logos, watermark, border, extra objects, device frames, or presentation sheet. Produce exactly one final square icon.
